Site Plan for Final Project

Site Name:

Willard Bike Club

This website is intended to inform Willard bikers about events and trails they can attend/use.

Site Purpose:

This website has upcoming events, such as races, and current trails they can use. It also includes nearby bike shops.

Scenarios:

  • What biking events are nearby that I can train for or go to?
  • Where are the best places or best trails I can use for recreational biking here?
  • Where could I get bike supplies or repairs nearby?
