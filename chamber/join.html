Membership Selection

GOLD

Gold Membership

Price: $35 per month

Perks include:

  • Special Chamber events, such as the annual Maddox restaurant trip.
  • Newspaper section for your organization.
  • Annual leadership training.
  • Premium advertising on newsletters and chamber website.
  • 15% discount at special events.
  • 15% discount at Willard businesses for all those in your organization.

SILVER

Silver Membership

Price: $25 per month

Perks include:

  • Special Chamber events, such as the annual Maddox restaurant trip.
  • Newspaper section for your organization.
  • Annual leadership training.
  • Advertising on newsletters and chamber website.
  • 10% discount at special events.
  • 10% discount for Willard businesses.

BRONZE

Bronze Membership

Price: $15 per month

Perks include:

  • Special Chamber events, such as the annual Maddox restaurant trip.
  • Newspaper section for your organization.
  • Annual leadership training.
  • Advertising in the newsletter and limited chamber website advertising.
  • 5% discount at special events.

NON-PROFIT

Non-Profit Membership

Price: $0

Perks include:

  • Special Chamber events, such as the annual Maddox restaurant trip.
  • Newspaper section for your organization.
  • Limited advertising in the newsletter and limited chamber website advertising.
